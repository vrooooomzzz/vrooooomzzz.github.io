# Duc Tuan Portfolio - Personas

These are functional archetypes for the approved portfolio, not invented biographies or separate authenticated roles.

## P1: Evidence-seeking reader

- **Who**: an admissions reviewer, internship mentor, or potential collaborator evaluating Duc Tuan's work.
- **Motivation**: understand his mathematical foundation, analytical methods, contribution, and ability to communicate findings.
- **Needs**: a clear introduction; concise education and experience; three specific project narratives; correctly labeled course certificates; readable mobile and keyboard navigation.
- **Constraints**: limited review time, varying technical familiarity, and no access to private credentials or employer data. Contact details and project URLs may be unavailable.
- **Success**: can explain what Duc Tuan worked on and distinguish supported evidence, team contributions, and recommendations from unverified claims.
- **Journey**: introduction, background, selected projects, skills/certificates; section navigation remains available without a mandatory sequence.
- **Stories**: DT-US-01 through DT-US-07.

## P2: Portfolio owner or trusted maintainer

- **Who**: Duc Tuan or an authorized person maintaining the static portfolio.
- **Motivation**: keep the public site accurate, usable, and free of private credentials.
- **Needs**: a publication allowlist, separate local working inputs, clear missing-content rules, and repeatable privacy/build verification.
- **Constraints**: GitHub source and bundled assets are public when published; hiding a link does not protect a file. Existing history may contain earlier owner content and must be assessed separately.
- **Success**: can identify every publishable asset and verify that raw inputs and private records do not enter a new public change.
- **Journey**: edit source-grounded content, inspect proposed evidence, verify publication boundary and working navigation, then follow the separately approved publication process.
- **Stories**: DT-US-08; reviews the content in DT-US-01 through DT-US-06.

## Compliance

The personas introduce no account, analytics tracking, contact collection, or administrative interface. Security Baseline and PBT are disabled and skipped. Mandatory privacy requirements apply to both archetypes.
